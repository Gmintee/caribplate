Ask anyone in Trinidad about breakfast on the go and the answer is likely to be doubles. Soft, golden bara folded around curried channa, finished with a spoonful of sauce and a little pepper if you dare. It is quick, cheap and hard to stop at one, which raises a fair question: what is actually in it?

The short answer is about 280 calories per double. The longer answer is more interesting, because where those calories come from explains why doubles feel so filling.

Where the calories come from

Protein 13%Carbs 59%Fat 28%

Most of the energy in a double comes from the fried bara. The channa adds plant protein and fibre, which is why a double tends to keep you fuller than its calories might suggest.

Most of the energy comes from the bara. The channa brings the protein and the fibre.

Can doubles fit into your day?

Yes. The useful question is not whether a food is good or bad but how it sits in your daily total. Two doubles is 560 calories, roughly a third of a 1,650 calorie day. If you know your own daily number, you can plan the rest of the day around it and keep eating the food you love.

Doubles calories: common questions

How many calories are in one double?

About 280 calories, with roughly 9g of protein, 42g of carbohydrate, 9g of fat and 5g of fibre.

How many calories are in two doubles?

About 560 calories, with roughly 18g of protein, 84g of carbohydrate, 18g of fat and 10g of fibre.

Are doubles healthy?

Doubles are mainly carbohydrate from the fried bara, with plant protein and fibre from the channa. At 280 calories each, one or two can fit into most eating plans if you keep the rest of the day's food in mind.

Do pepper, chutney and other sauces add calories?

A little. Tamarind sauce, chutney and kuchela add a small amount, mostly from sugar, and how much goes on varies by vendor.

Where do these numbers come from?

They are Carib Plate's estimates for a standard serving, not laboratory measurements. Use them as a guide.
